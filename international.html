Definition

“General government” is the IMF’s internationally comparable government perimeter. It is broader than the Commonwealth-only gross-debt measure on the main Australian chart and may include central, state, local and social-security government entities, subject to each country’s reporting framework.

Source and scope

Embedded data use the April 2026 IMF World Economic Outlook vintage and indicator GGXWDG_NGDP. WEO periods retain country-specific annual and fiscal-year conventions. Values from 2026 onward are excluded because they are projections in this vintage. Taiwan is displayed using a shorter label than the IMF source naming convention.
