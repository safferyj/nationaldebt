nationaldebt.au: a data-first review

The published Commonwealth series reconciles with its cited source vintages. The principal risks are provenance and export completeness for readers, and a keyboard accessibility blocker for visitors.

Reviewed nationaldebt.au and repository commit 8e0a1cd5fa444938ab8786cfb2314367354d7320. The deployed and tracked index.html had the same SHA-256: cc299cc0cd8a1f270e72a7eac7f5986b7e810b0c8865ae4960d66c05e4d0f6c9.

A / Highest-weighted area

Correctness of data and citations

Result: the embedded historical values match the exact PBO and ABS publications identified on the site. The two 2002–03 debt/GDP exceptions are justified corrections to inconsistent PBO cells, not transcription mistakes. Agreement with a cited workbook is not proof that the underlying official series is error-free or up to date.

The audit compared 661 numeric PBO cells and 52 unavailable fiscal-balance cells across 55 financial years; independently recalculated 54 gross-debt growth percentages and 249 non-null GDP ratios; checked 55 June CPI readings and 55 four-quarter CPI averages; and matched 55 population denominators. There were zero unexplained site-to-source differences. The downloaded live CSV independently matched 1,316 numeric values and 59 intentional blanks across 55 rows and 25 numeric columns. No full browser test suite was run.

Verified source exceptions and definitions

The PBO workbook repeats its 2000–01 debt/GDP ratios in two 2002–03 cells even though the 2002–03 GDP and debt amounts differ. The site documents and recalculates exactly these cells:

Net debt / GDPTable 2 AL7: 6.6033%46,802 / 804,444 × 100 = 5.8179%5.8179%

PBO Table 2 notes confirm that gross debt is AGS on issue, including some securities held on behalf of states and the Northern Territory; the site's definition says so. Table 1 note (d) confirms the special treatment of Future Fund earnings in the underlying cash balance. Table 2 note (g) calls the “interest paid on AGS” line all general-government cash interest payments, including AGS; the site correctly avoids claiming it is AGS-only interest. Debt change, growth against prior debt, debt/GDP burden change, cash balance and fiscal balance are kept distinct. Fiscal balances before 1996–97 remain unavailable rather than invented.

The 20 embedded political segments cover all 55 year-end dates. All 55 Treasurer assignments reconcile with Treasury ministerial terms at 30 June, after normalising honorifics/name spelling and using the contemporaneous Treasury archive for 2012–13. That 28 June 2013 snapshot explicitly names Chris Bowen Treasurer from 27 June and Wayne Swan through 27 June; today's Treasury list instead says Bowen began 27 July. The site explicitly records this conflict and chooses the contemporary evidence. Prime-minister succession was checked against the National Archives overview; individual party and ministerial appointments were not exhaustively re-proven against every historical instrument.

D1 / source freshness Low impact, confirmed

The 2024–25 per-person denominator uses a superseded, explicitly identified ABS vintage

The site cites ABS December 2025 population Table 1. It correctly embeds 30 June 2025 ERP L187 = 27,611.0 thousand (27,611,000 people). ABS published the March 2026 release on 17 September 2026; its corresponding cell is 27,601.7 thousand. Of the site's 45 annual denominators from 1981 to 2025, only 2025 changed between these two releases.

For example, the 2024–25 gross-debt-per-person result moves from A$33,632.94 (site vintage) to A$33,644.27 (newer vintage), a difference of A$11.33/person or approximately 0.034%. Other 2024–25 per-person views are affected too; debt/GDP is not. This is a dated-vintage choice, not a transcription error. Rebase the 2025 denominator when updating the snapshot, and display an “as of” date beside per-capita views.

D2 / exported data Medium impact, confirmed

“Complete dataset, including all derived fields” overstates what the CSV contains

The live Download all data (CSV) file has 55 records and 29 columns. Its 25 numeric columns reconcile with the site, but there are no CPI indexes or conversion factors, no inflation-adjusted dollar series, and no real per-capita or real-flow results. A user cannot reproduce the page's 2024–25-dollar charts from the download alone, despite the visible download description. It also emits unrounded floating-point artifacts, for example gross_debt_burden_change_pp = -0.42320000000000135 for 2024–25; this is a presentation defect, not a different calculation.

Recommendation: either narrow the claim to “all years and selected nominal/percentage measures” or export the two CPI inputs, the reference bases, source vintages, and the real and per-person measures actually plotted. Give derived CSV values a documented, sensible precision without rounding the underlying chart calculations.

D3 / citation durability Medium impact, confirmed

The CPI citation can silently change, and neither chart values nor CSV rows carry specific provenance

PBO and population inputs link to exact downloadable vintages. The CPI card instead points to ABS latest-release, which currently leads to July 2026. The actual verified series is A2325846C in 6401017.xlsx inside that release's CPI-Quarterly.zip. A later release will change the target without changing the frozen values embedded in the page. All 12 source cards are inside a closed Sources disclosure, and the CSV has no source/vintage or sheet-column metadata.

Recommendation: link the frozen CPI archive and identify its workbook/series directly; preserve the general ABS landing page as contextual information. Add a compact provenance map for fiscal, CPI and population fields, and include source IDs, dates and units with the CSV or a companion header. The cell map below is a ready-made starting point.

D4 / official-source residual Low impact, source caveat

1996–97 PBO cash balances have a $2m source-internal subtotal difference

PBO Table 1 reports receipts AF8 = 133,592 and payments AF17 = 139,689 ($m): their difference is −6,097, versus the reported underlying cash balance AF23 = −6,099. Adding policy-purpose cash flows AF26 = 7,241 gives a headline subtotal of 1,144 versus the reported AF29 = 1,142. All 55 underlying-cash and 55 headline-cash identities and 29 historical fiscal-balance identities were inspected: elsewhere the residual is at most $1m, consistent with publication in rounded $m. The reason for the two $2m residuals in 1996–97 was not established.

The site correctly transcribes the published balances; this is not a site-to-source discrepancy. Document the source caveat when automating reconciliation, and consult the original Budget papers before substituting calculated subtotals for the published series.

B / Live website

User experience and accessibility

Working in sampled browsers The deployed page loaded with ten measure controls, a populated chart, working year/view selection, a deep-linked 2002–03 real-per-capita view, share feedback and a 55-row download. Desktop Chromium and portrait iPhone WebKit / Pixel Chromium emulations had no initial page or console errors and no horizontal overflow. These are sampled observations, not a full device or accessibility certification.

U1 / keyboard blocker High impact, confirmed

Tab is intercepted before the first focusable element; Space cannot activate a focused button

The document-level shortcut listener calls preventDefault() for Tab to cycle chart lenses and for Space to move the legend, regardless of focus. On the live desktop page, the first Tab left document.activeElement on BODY and switched to Dollars; a second Tab still did not move focus. After focusing the previous-year button programmatically, Space left the year unchanged and moved the legend instead. The stylesheet also applies outline: none !important to all focused elements, with no replacement focus ring; the skip link has no visible-on-focus positioning rule.

Impact: keyboard-only visitors cannot reach normal page navigation, Sources, Download or chart controls by Tab, and lose native Space-to-activate behaviour. This conflicts with basic keyboard and visible-focus accessibility. Recommendation: reserve Tab and Space for the browser, scope non-native shortcuts to an appropriate chart context, ignore editable fields, add :focus-visible indicators and show the skip link on focus. Test the actual focus order and native button activation, not only the custom shortcut effect. See focus CSS and shortcut handler.

U2 / fiscal scope Medium impact, interpretation risk

The first visible screen does not say this is Commonwealth debt, not all Australian public debt

At the top of the live page a sighted visitor sees the nationaldebt.au wordmark and the debt chart, with no visible page heading or Commonwealth-scope caption. The accurate explanation is in collapsed Definitions/Usage content and the chart's non-visual ARIA label. A national-sounding domain plus a single gross-debt figure can reasonably be mistaken for debt of all Australian governments. Add a visible heading/caption such as “Commonwealth gross AGS on issue (including AGS held on behalf of states/NT); not total state and local debt” immediately adjacent to the chart, with a short link to Definitions. Do not broaden the dataset silently.

U3 / print Medium impact, confirmed

Printing suppresses every meaningful section

The print CSS hides the chart, Definitions, Sources, Usage, Download and About while expecting retired hero/detail/table content to remain. The current page has none of those retired elements. With print media enabled on the deployed desktop page, no visible main section remained; only the offscreen skip-link text was exposed to text extraction. Provide a printable data summary or table and keep definitions/source provenance visible, or explicitly offer a dedicated print view.

U4 / narrow touch landscape Medium impact, confirmed

The rotate notice hides sources and CSV as well as the chart

At 851 × 393 CSS pixels with a coarse pointer, the landscape media rule applies body > *:not(.landscape-warning) { display: none !important; }. The chart, navigation, definitions, sources and export become inaccessible until rotation. The chart may genuinely need height, but a read-only fallback should preserve definitions, citations and download for users who cannot rotate a device. This was browser emulation, not a physical-device measurement.

C + D / Repository

Maintainability by coding agents and test framework

Strengths: no application framework, chart library or runtime data fetch; the site works offline. Fiscal, CPI, population and political snapshots are clearly grouped; missing CPI/population throws rather than silently filling values; source cards distinguish direct data from context. popup-text.md is the editable source for generated popup copy, and scripts/build.js validates sections and links. Browser suites exercise substantial touch/mouse interaction, layouts and error handling.

M1 / agent handoff Medium impact, confirmed

The canonical agent instructions and legacy styles describe UI that no longer exists

AGENTS.md and .github/copilot-instructions.md say the body has selected-year details, insights and a table, and that renderAll() refreshes them. In this commit, renderAll() only refreshes controls/measure rows/year/chart/label fitting; there is no table, hero, detail or insight DOM. The instructions also refer to sourceLink(), which is absent from the current page. The stylesheet still contains rules for those absent components, contributing to the blank print view. The 4,926-line single-file application also requires agents to coordinate positional 16-element fiscal arrays (15 documented fields plus an ignored legacy false), labels, CSV, definitions, chart series, generated popups and hard-coded year copy.

Recommendation: update both instruction files to describe the current chart-only UI; remove retired CSS after confirming the archive stays untouched. Keep the delivered page offline and framework-free, but consider a build-time schema/provenance manifest that generates or validates the embedded arrays and year text. Keep index.html as the deployable artifact; do not introduce runtime fetching just to ease editing.

T1 / missing data gates Medium impact, confirmed

Browser tests do not protect the figures, citations, CSV fields or native keyboard access

With Playwright 1.63.0, playwright.config.cjs enumerates 200 installed mobile descriptors in this environment (90 Apple/WebKit; 110 other/Chromium) and four desktop profiles. Smoke and interaction specs cover rendering, layout, gestures, shares and CSV filename. There is no committed reconciliation of fiscal cells/CPI averages/ERP vintages, derived ratios, 2002–03 exception, political transition or CSV header/record values. The shortcut test asserts lens cycling with Tab but never asserts that Tab moves focus or that Space activates a focused button.

Recommendation: add a quick, offline data-contract check using pinned official snapshots/fixtures with documented hashes, column maps, tolerance and an explicit two-cell exception; assert representative real/nominal chart outputs, all CSV headers and numeric formulas, plus keyboard tab order and Space/Enter activation. Run this small check on every change; reserve the 200-device interaction matrix for release/nightly coverage. A copied external workbook alone is not a replacement for a clearly versioned provenance manifest.

T2 / delivery checks Low impact, confirmed

No tracked CI workflow or non-mutating generated-copy check

There is no tracked .github/workflows/ job. The npm test default runs mobile smoke only; npm run test:all opts into the full matrices. npm run build rewrites index.html if popup-text.md is newer, rather than offering a separate --check mode to fail on stale generated copy. Add a fast CI job for data contracts, HTML/build consistency and targeted browser checks, with a separately scheduled/full device matrix. This is a process gap, not evidence that the current page fails its existing suites.

Method, source fingerprints and limits

Review date: 25 September 2026, Australia/Sydney. The live document and tracked index.html matched byte for byte. Public official workbooks were downloaded directly from the URLs below and read from their XLSX cells. Fiscal $m values had to match exactly; published four-decimal percentages and the independently derived growth series were checked within 0.000051 percentage points. All 249 populated GDP percentages were independently checked against their dollar amounts. The only intentional deviations from PBO ratios were the two 2002–03 cells recorded above. Source-internal cash and fiscal subtotals were also inspected separately from site-to-source transcription. Official ABS population was converted from thousands to people; June-quarter CPI was used for debt and the average of September, December, March and June for flows. The live CSV was parsed separately and 25 numeric columns were recalculated using the embedded source inputs, allowing only normal binary floating-point tolerances.
