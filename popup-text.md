# Interactive measure pop-up text

# Edit the Markdown between the named section markers below, then run
# `npm run build`. The generated `index.html` contains both formatted tooltip
# HTML and plain text for browser titles and accessibility labels.
#
# Supported authoring features are paragraphs and line breaks, **bold** and
# *italic* emphasis, `inline code`, and links such as
# [an official source](https://www.pbo.gov.au/). Raw HTML is not interpreted.

[gross]
**Gross debt** is the face value of Australian Government Securities (AGS) on issue. It is a debt amount measured at 30 June. The PBO notes that total AGS on issue can include securities held on behalf of the states and the Northern Territory.
[/gross]

[net]
**Net debt** is interest-bearing liabilities less selected financial assets, as defined in the PBO historical workbook. It is a debt amount measured at 30 June.
[/net]

[ucb]
The **underlying cash balance** is the Commonwealth's cash-based Budget result. The PBO defines it as receipts less payments, less net Future Fund earnings from 2005-06 to 2019-20; in other years it is receipts less payments. Positive values are surpluses and negative values are deficits. It is a flow measure, not a debt amount or change in debt amount.
[/ucb]

[fiscal]
The **fiscal balance** is an accrual-based Budget result calculated from revenue, expenses and net capital investment. It records economic activity when it arises, including some non-cash items and timing differences, so it can differ from the underlying cash balance. Positive values are surpluses and negative values are deficits. The embedded PBO series begins in 1996-97.
[/fiscal]

[grossChange]
**Gross debt change** is the difference between consecutive gross debt amounts. In the % of GDP view it is divided by current-year nominal GDP; in dollar views it is shown as a dollar difference between amounts or per-capita equivalent. It is not the Budget surplus or deficit.
[/grossChange]

[grossGrowth]
**Gross debt growth versus prior debt** is the derived percentage (`(current gross debt - previous gross debt) / previous gross debt * 100`). It measures the percentage change in the gross debt amount, not the change in the debt-to-GDP ratio. It is available in the % of GDP view only.
[/grossGrowth]

[netChange]
**Net debt change** is the difference between consecutive net debt amounts. In the % of GDP view it is divided by current-year nominal GDP; in dollar views it is shown as a dollar difference between amounts or per-capita equivalent. It is not the Budget surplus or deficit.
[/netChange]

[agsInterest]
The [PBO table](https://www.pbo.gov.au/publications-and-data/data-and-tools/data-portal/historical-fiscal-data) calls this “Interest paid on AGS on issue”, but its note (g) defines it as all cash interest payments of the general government sector, including those relating to AGS on issue. It is not reduced by interest receipts. In the % of GDP view it is shown as a share of nominal GDP.
[/agsInterest]

[interest]
**Net interest payments** are the PBO measure of interest paid minus interest receipts. Interest receipts can include receipts from cash and deposits, advances and loans, investments and placements, and other interest-earning financial assets. It is an annual flow and is shown as a share of nominal GDP in the percentage view.
[/interest]

[government]
**Government** shows or hides the political-party band beneath the chart. It is a visual guide to the Commonwealth government in power at the end of each financial year; it does not remove the political information from the archived reference page or tooltips.
[/government]
